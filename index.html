TIME SINCE WE LAST MET

Since that last moment...

0 DAYS
0 HOURS
0 MINUTES
0 SECONDS
03 JUNE 2026 · 12:00 PM
❤️

Pata nahi tum yeh dekh rahi ho ya nahi…
pata nahi tum kabhi is page tak aaogi bhi ya nahi…

Lekin agar kabhi aa jao, toh bas ek baar is counter ko dekhna.
Itne din ho gaye hain… aur main aaj bhi wahi hoon.

Pata nahi kyun, but I miss you… bahut zyada.

Kabhi-kabhi bina kisi reason ke tumhari yaad aa jaati hai. Koi song sunta hoon, koi purani photo dekh leta hoon, koi chhoti si baat hoti hai… aur pata nahi kaise, baat phir tum tak pahunch jaati hai.

Sabse ajeeb baat pata hai kya hai?

Mujhe ab bhi roz lagta hai ki shayad aaj tumhara message aa jayega.

Phone uthata hoon toh ek second ke liye mann karta hai notifications check karun…
shayad tumne kuch bheja ho.
Shayad tumne bas “hi” likha ho.
Shayad tumne kuch bhi nahi likha ho… bas mera naam tumhare chat mein khula ho.

Aur phir jab kuch nahi hota na…
toh ek ajeeb sa silence reh jaata hai.

Pehle mujhe lagta tha kisi ko miss karna matlab bas uski yaad aana hota hai.

Ab samajh aaya hai ki kisi ko khona kaisa hota hai.

Kisi aise insaan ko, jiske message ka tumhe bina soche intezaar hota tha…
jiski ek call tumhara poora mood change kar deti thi…
jiske saath baat karna tumhari daily life ka normal sa part ban gaya tha…

aur phir ek din woh sab normal nahi rehta.

Tumhari kami mujhe ab unhi chhoti-chhoti cheezon mein feel hoti hai.

Kabhi mann karta hai tumhe koi random si baat bataun…
phir yaad aata hai ki ab main woh random baat tumhe bata nahi sakta.

Kabhi koi photo dekh kar smile aati hai…
phir dil karta hai tumhe bhejun…
phir yaad aata hai ki ab woh bhi nahi kar sakta.

Aur shayad isi ko miss karna kehte hain…

jab insaan sirf yaad nahi aata,
balki tumhari roz ki chhoti-chhoti aadaton mein rehne lagta hai.

Sakshi, mujhe nahi pata tumhare dil mein mere liye ab kya hai.
Mujhe nahi pata tum kabhi yeh sab padhogi bhi ya nahi.

Lekin ek baat sach hai—

maine tumhe jitna miss kiya hai na, utna shayad maine kabhi imagine bhi nahi kiya tha.

Aur iss counter mein jo bhi number badhta ja raha hai, mere liye woh sirf days nahi hain…

woh utne din hain jitne din se main tumhare ek message ka intezaar kar raha hoon.

Aur pata hai?

Aaj bhi dil ke kisi kone mein woh chhoti si hope hai…

“Aaj shayad uska message aa hi jayega.”

Maybe tomorrow.
Maybe someday.

Bas…
main abhi bhi wait kar raha hoon. ❤️
